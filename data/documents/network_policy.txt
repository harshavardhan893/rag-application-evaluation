
NETWORK AND INTERNET USAGE POLICY

1. Wi-Fi Access
Employees can connect to the corporate Wi-Fi network using their company credentials.
Personal devices must connect to the guest Wi-Fi network.
Employees must not share corporate Wi-Fi passwords with unauthorized individuals.

2. VPN Access
Employees working remotely must connect to the company VPN to access internal resources.
Multi-factor authentication is required when connecting to the VPN.
Employees should disconnect from the VPN when their work session ends.

3. Internet Usage
The company internet connection is intended primarily for work-related activities.
Accessing malicious websites or downloading unauthorized files is prohibited.
Employees must report suspicious links or phishing messages to the IT security team.

4. Network Issues
Employees experiencing network connectivity problems should restart their device and check their Wi-Fi connection.
If the issue continues, they should submit a support ticket to the IT helpdesk.
Critical network outages must be reported immediately.

5. Data Security
Employees must not transfer confidential company information through personal email accounts.
Sensitive files should only be shared through company-approved platforms.
