
DATA SECURITY POLICY

1. Data Classification
Company information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.
Employees must handle information according to its classification.
Confidential and Restricted information must only be accessed by authorized employees.

2. Data Backup
Important work files must be stored on company-approved storage platforms.
Employees should not rely on personal USB drives for storing important company data.
The IT department performs regular backups of company systems.

3. Phishing Prevention
Employees must not open suspicious email attachments or click unknown links.
Suspicious emails must be reported to the IT security team.
Employees should never share passwords or verification codes through email.

4. Lost Devices
Employees must immediately report lost or stolen company devices to the IT helpdesk.
The IT team may remotely disable access to company accounts to protect company information.

5. Data Sharing
Confidential company information must only be shared with authorized recipients.
Employees must use approved company platforms when sharing sensitive files.
