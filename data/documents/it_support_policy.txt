
IT SUPPORT POLICY

1. Support Hours
The IT helpdesk operates Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
The helpdesk is closed on Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays.

2. Password Reset
Employees who forget their account password can request a password reset through the IT helpdesk portal.
For security reasons, employees must verify their identity before a password reset is approved.
Temporary passwords must be changed immediately after the first login.

3. Account Lockout
An account is automatically locked after five consecutive incorrect password attempts.
Employees must contact the IT helpdesk to unlock their accounts.
The helpdesk verifies the employee's identity before unlocking an account.

4. Response Time
High-priority issues receive an initial response within two business hours.
Medium-priority issues receive an initial response within eight business hours.
Low-priority issues receive an initial response within two business days.

5. Software Installation
Employees must submit a software installation request through the IT helpdesk portal.
Only software approved by the organization's IT department may be installed on company devices.
